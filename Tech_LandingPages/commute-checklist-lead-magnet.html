Is Your Office
Worth the Commute?

A 12-point checklist for tech leaders who want their office to be the reason — not the obstacle — for coming in.

If people are commuting to your office, it has to be worth the trip. Here are the 12 things that make it so.

Your score
0 / 12
01 Focus & Deep Work

If the office doesn't enable better work than home, there's no case for the commute.

There is dedicated quiet space Not just a "please use headphones" policy — actual physical separation from noise.
The desk setup is noticeably better than home Monitor size, chair quality, keyboard — not just "a desk."
Natural light reaches most workstations Or lighting has been deliberately designed, not defaulted.
02 Collaboration & Energy

The commute is worth it when the office creates something that can't happen remotely — and that starts with how people work together.

There are informal collision points Areas where people naturally run into each other — not just scheduled meeting rooms.
There is a range of collaboration spaces beyond the boardroom Standing tables, lounge areas, open project zones.
The office has energy, not just occupancy People want to be there, not just tolerating it. You can feel the difference.
03 Comfort & Amenities

The details are what make people feel the commute was worth it — or not.

Temperature and acoustics are controlled Your team isn't complaining about noise or cold every week.
There is access to good coffee and food On-site or close enough that people actually use it, not just endure it.
People have somewhere to put their things Lockers, storage, personal space — not hauling a bag every time they come in.
04 Design & Signal

The office has to look and feel like somewhere worth going. If it doesn't have a point of view, it doesn't have a case.

The office has a clear design point of view Not generic, not default — a space that clearly had thought put into it.
You'd be comfortable showing a client around without warning The space reflects well on the company without preparation.
People have mentioned the office unprompted When people tell others about your office without being asked, it's working.

How to read your score

10 – 12 Your office is worth the commute. It's giving people a genuine reason to come in — and they can feel it.
7 – 9 Your office is close. A few gaps are making the commute harder to justify than it should be.
4 – 6 Your office is breaking even. It's not losing you talent yet — but it's not winning any either.
0 – 3 Your office is working against you. If people can get their best work done at home, they will.
